Plyometric progression

Ground contacts per session, inside Day A. Each step up is gated on your right side staying quiet — the gate is a requirement, not a suggestion. If symptoms appear, drop back one level and hold there for three weeks.

Seven rules

  1. The right shin and calf outrank the plan. Symptoms mean the session stops, not that you push through.
  2. Add one run at a time, four weeks apart. Never add a run and raise volume in the same week.
  3. Volume rises no more than 10% per week, with a down week every fourth.
  4. Two quality sessions per week, maximum. As volume grows, only the easy running grows.
  5. Hard sessions are steered by heart rate. Pace is the sanity check, not the target.
  6. Day A and Day B happen every week, unchanged. Cut running before you cut strength.
  7. No racing in this block. The Week 12 benchmark is a repeat of a Week 1 run, not a time trial.
